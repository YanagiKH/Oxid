## Evidence boundary

Each host first verifies raw comparator outcomes at original same-host paths.
The compact capsule contains unchanged compressed raw observations, ordinary
lifecycle counterparts, rosters, input/build/plan/result bindings, small required
comparison artifacts, controlled failure logs, and a closed member inventory.
The compact archive is capped below32MiB. Final joining validates this transported
closure and actual raw row/count identities; it does not infer execution from a
summary or claim that hashes are cryptographic proof of execution.

and cache directories are safely regenerable and are not evidence archives;
retained produced executables, recipes and raw observations are preserved.
Compact capsules disclose this replay boundary explicitly. Failure packaging runs
on `always()` and never changes a failed qualification status into a success.
